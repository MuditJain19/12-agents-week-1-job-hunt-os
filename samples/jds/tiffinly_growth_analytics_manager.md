# SAMPLE JOB (fictional company): Growth Analytics Manager, Tiffinly, Bengaluru
Tiffinly is a home-cooked meal subscription app in 12 Indian cities.

**You will**
- Own the analytics behind acquisition, activation and retention for our subscription product.
- Design and read A/B tests on onboarding, pricing and referral offers.
- Build cohort and funnel views the growth team uses every week.
- Lead a team of 3 to 4 analysts.
- Use LLM tools to speed up insight generation.

**You have**
- 4 to 7 years in growth or product analytics, ideally consumer apps.
- Strong SQL; experience with BigQuery or a similar warehouse.
- Experience running experiments at scale.
- Nice to have: Python, referral or loyalty programmes, fraud controls.
